01 / Engagements

Three moments when senior technical judgment changes the outcome.

Focused scopes around a decision—not project labor, seat-filling, or an open-ended transformation program.

01 — Before money moves

Technical diligence

An independent read on the product, platform, team, and hidden liabilities behind an investment, acquisition, or strategic bet.

  • Architecture and codebase risk review
  • Security, identity, and integration exposure
  • Team and delivery capability assessment
  • Decision memo with red flags and conditions
02 — While the stakes rise

Fractional architecture advisor

Senior technical counsel for a founder, board, or leadership team navigating scale, enterprise demands, and irreversible platform choices.

  • Architecture decision support
  • Enterprise readiness and roadmap pressure-test
  • API, identity, and security strategy
  • Technical hiring and vendor review
03 — When confidence breaks

Build audit

A fast, unsentimental review when the demo looks good but delivery feels fragile, the roadmap keeps slipping, or the system will not scale.

  • Production-readiness assessment
  • Delivery bottleneck diagnosis
  • Integration and dependency mapping
  • Prioritized recovery plan
